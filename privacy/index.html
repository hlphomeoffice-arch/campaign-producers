Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Campaign Producers handles information submitted by people applying to its professional talent network.

Information we collect

We collect the personal, professional, portfolio, rate, availability and consent information entered in the application form. This includes your country, city and the time-zone setting detected from your device. We do not request references at the initial application stage.

How we use it

We use the information to assess potential professional fit, organise applications, invite suitable applicants to introductory conversations and contact applicants about relevant paid project opportunities. If you separately opt into the newsletter, we also use your email address to send Campaign Producers news and updates.

Storage and access

Applications are recorded in Google Workspace. Access is limited to authorised Campaign Producers personnel and service providers supporting the application workflow.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as it remains reasonably relevant to the talent network, legal obligations and the purposes described above. Records are reviewed periodically and removed when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You may ask Campaign Producers to correct or delete your application information, or to stop contacting you about opportunities. You may unsubscribe from newsletter messages at any time. Email henk@campaignproducers.com for a privacy request.
