Creator content can win attention and still leave the brand behind. Kantar analysed more than 15,000 branded creator assets across TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram. Only 6 per cent delivered both strong platform engagement and strong brand-building potential. That is not an argument against creators. It is a warning against treating engagement as the campaign result. The answer is not a tighter script. It is a better production system: • Give the creator one precise audience job. • Protect their voice with clear, limited guardrails. • Agree usage rights and asset versions before filming. • Measure brand effect and commercial action alongside platform response. Creators are not ad placements. The useful work happens when their relationship with an audience is connected to a clear brand purpose.
