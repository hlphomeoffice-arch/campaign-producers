Brand and performance are one campaign, not two budgets.

When money gets tight, performance usually wins the argument. It has dashboards, leads and a cost per acquisition. Brand arrives with a story about future value.

But performance captures demand that already exists. It cannot, by itself, make enough people know you, understand you or prefer you before they are ready to buy.

When every advert asks for the sale, the campaign has skipped the reason to buy.

The answer is not a memorable campaign over here and a measurable campaign over there. Build one customer promise that works across the whole journey:

• Earn attention before the customer is buying.
• Build memory around the problem you solve.
• Prove the promise when they start comparing.
• Make the next step obvious when they are ready.

Different assets can do different jobs. The strategic spine should stay the same.

A campaign should not choose between making people care and making it easy to buy. It should do both, in the right sequence, under one idea.
