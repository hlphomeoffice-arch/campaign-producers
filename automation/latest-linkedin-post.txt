Creative feedback fails when every reaction is treated as a decision. One person is judging strategy. Another is correcting copy. Someone else is managing risk. The creative team receives all of it and is expected to find one direction. A better review starts before the work is opened. Name the decision required at that stage. Set the criteria. Separate specialist input from approval authority. Keep settled points settled. Then ask one accountable lead to resolve contradictions and issue a prioritised direction. If another round is needed, identify why. A changed strategy, absent approver or unclear brief cannot be repaired by adjusting the execution again. The aim is not to remove useful opinions. It is to turn them into a clear choice that protects the campaign idea, the people making it and the budget paying for it.
