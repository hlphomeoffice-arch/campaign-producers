Who we are looking for

Selective without the theatre.

The initial network is for established specialists rather than people entering the industry for the first time.

Clearly exceptional

You have a defined specialist discipline and a strong body of professional work that demonstrates judgement, not only execution.

Dependable by default

You communicate clearly, manage your time, meet commitments and raise risks early enough for the team to act.

Commercially aware

You care about what the work must achieve for the client, and you can distinguish a beautiful output from a valuable result.

Constructively creative

You collaborate well, bring your own ideas and respond to feedback without surrendering professional standards.

AI can support exceptional talent, but it cannot replace talent, judgement, care or commitment. Technology must be used transparently and within each client’s confidentiality, intellectual-property and approval requirements.
