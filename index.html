01 / The false choice

The problem

Finding talent is easy. Building the right team isn’t.

Most businesses face three options: hire a full agency, coordinate a loose group of freelancers or ask an overstretched internal team to do too much.

One can be expensive. Another becomes fragmented. The third becomes exhausting.

01 / Fixed agency

A full agency can mean paying for more than the campaign needs.

You may inherit extra layers, roles and costs before the clearest route to the result has been worked out.

02 / Loose freelancers

Good people, but no single campaign direction.

You source, brief and coordinate every contributor while trying to keep the work connected yourself.

03 / Internal team

More pressure on people already carrying the business.

Important campaign decisions compete with the work your team must keep delivering every day.

Our alternative

Only the talent your campaign needs, assembled around one clear commercial objective and led as one team.

05 / Human + AI

People make the decisions. AI earns its place.

Human-led.

Smarter by design.

AI can make parts of campaign development and production faster, more flexible and more affordable.

We use it when it creates real value, including research, exploration, pre-production, production, adaptation and versioning.

But technology does not decide what your audience should feel, which story is worth telling or who should be trusted to tell it. That judgement remains human.

Where AI could add value, its role is discussed as part of the agreed production approach.

10 / Questions

Before you begin

Clear answers. No hidden assumptions.

Is Campaign Producers an agency?

Campaign Producers provides the leadership and accountability of an agency without forcing every brief through the same permanent team. Specialists are chosen around the campaign.

Can you work with our existing team or agency?

Yes. We can work with your internal team, existing agency or trusted suppliers and add only the skills that are missing.

Can we choose the people working on our campaign?

Yes. We recommend the strongest team for the brief and explain why each person has been selected. You approve the team before work begins.

How are specialists selected?

We consider the campaign objective, audience, format, budget, timing, creative approach and working style. Craft matters, but so do judgement, reliability and collaboration.

Do you use AI?

Yes, when it improves the work, reduces unnecessary cost or creates useful flexibility. Every campaign remains led by human strategy, creativity and accountability.

How much does it cost?

Every campaign is scoped individually. Your proposal separates strategy, talent, production, technology and management costs so you can see where the budget goes.

I am a creative specialist. Can I join?

You can apply to join the Collective. Every application is reviewed for quality, experience, reliability and fit with the work Campaign Producers is building.
